# Mercury Product Protection Platinum Plans and Prices Canada

Harris Boat Works publishes current Canadian-dollar retail pricing for Mercury Platinum Product Protection plans. Platinum is the highest Mercury Product Protection coverage tier and covers eligible mechanical and electrical failures under the plan contract.

## Direct answer

Mercury Platinum Product Protection at Harris Boat Works currently starts at $76 CAD for a one-year plan on a 2.5-14.9 HP outboard. A four-year plan for a 75-149.9 HP outboard is $1,748 CAD. The complete rate card ranges up to $22,469 CAD for a five-year plan on a 600 HP outboard. Prices are before HST and final eligibility and price are confirmed by serial number. Mercury defines Product Protection as an extended service contract, not an extension of the standard product warranty.

## Promotion-aware quote calculation

When an applicable Mercury promotion adds Gold years, the included baseline is factory warranty (3 years) plus those Gold years. To reach a combined total as Platinum, HBW first charges the Gold-to-Platinum upgrade for all included Gold years, then adds only the cumulative Platinum price for the extra years beyond that baseline. An upgrade-only choice at the included total charges only the Gold upgrade. Choosing no paid plan keeps factory-plus-Gold coverage at $0. Combined coverage never exceeds Mercury's 8-year maximum, described as factory 3 years plus up to 5 Platinum years, with the factory period counted separately. HBW confirms current coverage from the motor serial record; current promotional terms always defer to https://www.mercuryrepower.ca/promotions. Final eligibility is confirmed by serial number.

## Eligibility snapshot

- Purchase before the applicable Mercury factory limited warranty expires.
- Recreational use and fewer than 500 engine hours, subject to the complete Mercury plan terms.
- Manufactured in the current calendar year or four preceding calendar years, subject to the complete eligibility rules and serial-number verification.
- Transferable to a subsequent recreational-use purchaser when Mercury's transfer requirements are met.
- $50 deductible per claim under the current Canadian Platinum plan terms.
